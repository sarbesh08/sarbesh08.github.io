Profile

About Me

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, geospatial analysis, structural engineering, and post-disaster damage assessment.

I am interested in developing and applying computational methods that improve how building and infrastructure data are collected, analyzed, and interpreted following natural hazard events.

Areas of Focus

Research Interests

01

Artificial Intelligence

Application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computer vision to civil engineering and post-disaster assessment.

02

Geospatial Analysis

Use and evaluation of geospatial data and spatial methods for building, infrastructure, and natural hazard applications.

03

Damage Assessment

Data-driven assessment of building damage and performance following tornadoes and other natural hazard events.

04

Structural Engineering

Investigation of building and structural component performance under extreme loading and natural hazards.

Current Work

Selected Research Projects

01

Post-Disaster Damage Assessment

Developing computational approaches that integrate street-level imagery and geospatial information to support scalable, building-level post-disaster data collection and assessment.

02

Tornado Damage and Building Vulnerability

Analyzing detailed post-tornado building damage information to better understand building and component performance under extreme wind loading.

03

Building Footprint Data Uncertainty

Evaluating differences among large-scale building footprint datasets and examining their implications for geospatial and building-level applications.
