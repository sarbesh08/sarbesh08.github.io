About Me

My research interests lie at the intersection of natural hazard engineering, infrastructure resilience, geospatial data science, computer vision, and computational modeling.

I am interested in developing quantitative methods that improve post-disaster assessment, infrastructure vulnerability analysis, and resilience modeling.

```

Research Interests

Natural Hazards

Understanding infrastructure performance and damage mechanisms during tornadoes and other extreme events.

Infrastructure Resilience

Developing quantitative approaches for modeling recovery, vulnerability, and system-level resilience.

Geospatial Data Science

Studying spatial uncertainty and using geospatial datasets for infrastructure and hazard assessment.

Computer Vision

Using imagery, machine learning, and automated methods for building-level data extraction and damage assessment.

```
